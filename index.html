Featured Case Study

ARCH Enterprise Intranet Relaunch

A full platform transformation from discovery through sustained adoption for a national organization with 4,500 employees and 250 or more locations.

The Problem

The intranet had been running for 7 years with no governance, no content strategy, and no technical oversight. The platform had grown chaotic as the company scaled from a handful of locations to a national footprint. Employees actively avoided it. Search returned irrelevant results, navigation made no sense, and user data was being fed from three conflicting systems.

The Approach

Before touching a single page, I ran a structured discovery process. A 1,000-person employee survey surfaced that only 39% of employees used the platform weekly, and 36% found it difficult to find information. I facilitated focus groups, conducted platform analytics audits, and ran multi-part discovery sessions with the vendor to validate findings. From there I redesigned the full information architecture, established CMS governance frameworks, rebuilt the admin network, and led a 9-week structured rollout.

The Results

+70% Site Visits +65% Content Views +60% Searches +208% App and Tool Opens +46% Sessions

The sustained data tells the real story. Content views and app opens continued to grow after launch, indicating that the new information architecture and tool integrations created lasting behavioral change rather than a short-term curiosity bump.
